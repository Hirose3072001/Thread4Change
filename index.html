✦ OUR STORY • JOURNEY OF COMPASSION

From Little Drawings to Sustainable Change

Every child is an artist born with an innocent worldview. We connect that creative innocence to generous hearts.

THREADS FOR CHANGE started with a simple belief: every child has a story waiting to be heard, and every artwork can create meaningful value.

During a painting workshop at Tâm Đức Shelter, over 40 children poured vibrant colors, pure imagination, and their unique views of the world onto paper.

Instead of letting these artworks stay hidden, we brought them to life on high-quality canvas tote bags — merging youthful creativity with positive social change.
